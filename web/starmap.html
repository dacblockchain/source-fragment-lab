Star map

One 2,048-byte chunk becomes one sky: star positions, colours, nebulae and constellations all come from its bytes, so the same chunk always draws the same map.

Your fragment

Takes the next unused chunk and marks it used: once you share the picture, treat those bytes as public, so they must never also go into a key.

Re-drawing a chunk you already used shows the map is reproducible. Only chunks that are already used can be re-drawn.

Export PNG
Why raw bytes here? Art is meant to be seen, not kept secret, and mixing in your device's randomness would make the map impossible to reproduce. So this page uses the chunk as-is, expanded with HKDF. That is the exception to the rule, not the rule.
